STV_REVIEW_009 - Architecture connector routes obscured the flow
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Category: visual architecture readability/review omission. Owner: delegated
reviewer. State: resolved. Affected: both architecture Draw.io/SVG pairs and
their embedded architecture pages. Evidence: the owner identified overlapping
arrows; inspection confirmed software GRAPH-to-BUNDLE crossed DETAIL_BUILD,
and unconstrained automatic routing allowed flows to coincide or cross boxes.
Round 1 checked component/interface coverage and links but missed readability.
Impact: readers could not reliably follow sources, destinations and direction.
This violates the purpose of a usable architecture flow view despite mechanical
link validation passing. Closure criterion: every interface can be followed
visually without unrelated-box crossings, coincident runs or hidden labels
and arrowheads, at the published reading scale.

Correction: arranged the system flow vertically, reorganized the software
components into spaced build/browser flows, fixed connection ports and supplied
explicit orthogonal waypoints in dedicated corridors. Retained all 12 software
and three system interfaces and all 11 real Need links. Updated embedding
aspect ratios to the new exports. SVGs were regenerated by the shared Draw.io
exporter; no hand-authored SVG or published raster substitute was used.

Closure evidence: geometry checks found no connector traversing a box interior
or sharing a collinear segment with another interface. Actual browser-rendered
SVG screenshots and embedded-page views were visually inspected for flow,
labels, arrowheads and readable scale. All 11 published Need links were checked
again. Temporary screenshots are inspection aids only. Updated concept-creation
and its readiness checklist to prevent this defect, and concept-review and
its checklist to require actual rendered visual review and hold acceptance
when that evidence is missing. The prior review omission is retained here.
